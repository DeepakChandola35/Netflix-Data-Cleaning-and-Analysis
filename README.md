 🎬 Netflix Content Strategy Analysis | Data Analytics Project

 🚀 Project Overview

This project explores Netflix's content library to uncover strategic insights about content distribution, growth patterns, audience targeting, and global expansion trends.

Using real-world Netflix data, I performed **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization** to extract meaningful business insights.

The objective was not just to analyze data — but to interpret it from a business and decision-making perspective.

---

 📊 Dataset Summary

- 📁 Source: Kaggle Netflix Dataset
- 📌 Total Records: 8,790
- 📌 Total Features: 10
- 📆 Content Release Year Range: 1925 – 2021
- 📅 Content Added on Platform: 2008 – 2021

 Key Attributes:
- Type (Movie / TV Show)
- Director
- Country
- Rating
- Duration
- Genre
- Date Added
- Release Year

 📈 Key Analytical Insights

 🎥 Content Distribution
- 70% Movies
- 30% TV Shows  
Netflix’s catalog is heavily movie-dominated.

---

 📅 Rapid Expansion Phase
Content growth accelerated significantly after **2015**, peaking around **2019–2020**, showing aggressive content investment strategy.

---

 🌍 Global Content Distribution
Top content-producing countries:
1. United States  
2. India  
3. United Kingdom  
4. Pakistan  
5. Canada  

This reflects Netflix’s strong international expansion focus.

---

 🎯 Audience Targeting Strategy
Most frequent ratings:
- TV-MA
- TV-14
- TV-PG

Netflix primarily targets mature and young adult audiences.

---

 🎬 Genre Popularity

**Top Movie Genres:**
- International Movies
- Dramas
- Comedies
- Documentaries

**Top TV Genres:**
- International TV Shows
- TV Dramas
- Crime TV Shows
- Kids' TV

International and drama-based content dominates the platform.

 📊 Business Interpretation

This analysis suggests:

✔ Netflix strategically expanded content after 2015  
✔ Strong dominance in international and drama genres  
✔ Focus on mature audience segments  
✔ Significant global production partnerships  
✔ Balanced monthly content release strategy
